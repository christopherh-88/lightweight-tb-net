=== Architecture & Preprocessing Delta: Our Implementation vs. TB-Net (Wong et al., 2022) ===

--- Model Architecture ---

TB-Net (official, Wong et al.)
  Backbone:       Machine-designed via neural architecture search
  Key blocks:     PEPX (Projection-Expansion-Projection-eXtension) blocks
                  with depth-wise separable convolutions
  Self-attention: Attention condensers with learned query/key/value projections
  Parameters:     4.24 M
  MACs:           0.42 G (highly efficient)
  Framework:      TensorFlow 1.15 / Keras

Our implementation (LightweightTBNet)
  Backbone:       ResNet-18 (ImageNet pretrained via torchvision)
  Self-attention: AttentionCondenser (one self-attention block appended to backbone)
  Parameters:     11.51 M  (2.7x more than TB-Net)
  MACs:           ~1.8 G   (4.3x more than TB-Net)
  Framework:      PyTorch 2.x

Delta summary:
  Our model is heavier (11.5M vs 4.24M params) because we use an off-the-shelf
  ResNet-18 backbone rather than the custom PEPX architecture. The original TB-Net
  was designed via machine-driven architecture search to be lightweight; we
  approximate the self-attention mechanism but not the backbone efficiency.

  For the compression experiments (Phases 3), our teacher model is LightweightTBNet,
  not the official TB-Net. The student (MobileNetV3-Small, 1.52M params) is 8x
  smaller than TB-Net and 7.6x smaller than our teacher.

--- Preprocessing ---

TB-Net (official)
  1. Crop: remove 5% from each side, 25% from the bottom
           (bounding box: offset (11,11), size 168×202)
  2. Resize to 224×224
  3. Normalise pixel values to [0, 1] (divide by 255)
  4. Corner masking: zero-out top-left and top-right corners (r<45 and c<45 or c>178)
  5. Re-normalise: (img - min) / (max - min)
  6. Apply average-intensity mask: add 0.531 to masked corners
  Augmentation (train only): random crop, random flip, random brightness, random contrast

Our implementation
  1. Resize to 224×224 (no cropping)
  2. Random horizontal flip + rotation ±10° + colour jitter (train only)
  3. ToTensor (converts to [0,1])
  4. Normalise: mean=[0.531, 0.531, 0.531], std=[0.229, 0.229, 0.229]
  No corner masking

Delta summary:
  Our preprocessing does NOT replicate the TB-Net corner masking step.
  The corner mask removes artefacts from X-ray film edges; omitting it means
  our model is exposed to edge information that the original was shielded from.
  Additionally, our normalisation uses ImageNet-style mean/std subtraction
  (adapted to this dataset's mean intensity 0.531) rather than min-max scaling.
  These differences mean that the official TB-Net weights CANNOT be directly
  applied to our preprocessed images and vice versa.

--- Dataset ---

TB-Net (official)
  Source:    Kaggle "tuberculosis-tb-chest-xray-dataset" (Rahman et al.)
             Full dataset: 3,500 Normal + ~3,500 TB images
  Splits (from official CSVs):
    Train:   5,541  (2,782 Normal + 2,759 TB)
    Val:     693    (348 Normal + 345 TB)
    Test:    693    (348 Normal + 345 TB)
  Image naming: TB-XXXX.png (in data/ after create_dataset.py preprocessing)

Our data directory (as-downloaded)
  Images on disk: 3,500 Normal + 700 TB = 4,200 total
  File naming:    Tuberculosis-XXXX.png (Tuberculosis-1 to Tuberculosis-700)
  Effectively loaded for test:
    Normal: 348 / 348 (100%)
    TB:      69 / 345  (20%)   ← 276 TB test images are missing from disk

Root cause (confirmed by re-download):
  We re-downloaded the Kaggle dataset (June 2021 archive, 663 MB).
  The current Kaggle version contains exactly 700 TB images (Tuberculosis-1 to
  Tuberculosis-700) and 3,500 Normal images — matching what is on disk.
  The DarwinAI splits reference TB images numbered up to TB-3500, i.e., the
  dataset originally had 3,500 TB images.

  Reproducibility gap: The Kaggle dataset was reduced from 3,500 → 700 TB
  images between when DarwinAI published (2021) and the current version.
  The 2,800 missing TB images are no longer publicly available through Kaggle.
  This makes exact split reproduction impossible without access to the original
  data archive used by the DarwinAI team.

Effective test set (all available TB images):
  TB in test split with disk hits:  69 / 345  (Tuberculosis-2 through Tuberculosis-700)
  TB in val split with disk hits:   58 / 345
  TB in train split with disk hits: 555 / 2759

Implication for reported numbers:
  All accuracy/sensitivity/specificity/AUC results in our reports are computed
  on a test set of 417 images (348 Normal + 69 TB) rather than the intended
  693 (348 Normal + 345 TB). Results on this partial subset should not be
  directly compared to the paper's 693-image test set. Our model achieves
  100% sensitivity on the 69 available TB images.

Direct comparison is also not straightforward because:
  a) Our model architecture differs from TB-Net (cannot share weights)
  b) Our preprocessing differs (different normalisation and corner masking)
  c) The full 3,500-image TB dataset used for evaluation is no longer
     available on Kaggle (reduced to 700 images)
  To run TB-Net pretrained weights faithfully, one would need:
    1. Manual download of weights from Google Drive
    2. TensorFlow 1.15 installed
    3. The original 3,500-TB-image dataset (no longer publicly available)
    4. Run official eval.py with TBNetDSI preprocessing
